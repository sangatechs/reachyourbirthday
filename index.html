How to Find Out When You Will Reach Your 18th Birthday

Turning 18 is a significant milestone in many jurisdictions, marking legal adulthood and the beginning of new rights and responsibilities. Calculating the exact date of an 18th birthday is straightforward: it occurs exactly 18 years after the date of birth. Understanding how to perform this calculation and how much time remains until the milestone helps with planning applications, celebrations, and transitions.

Calculation Steps

1
Identify the complete date of birth — day, month, and year. This is the starting point for the calculation.
2
Add 18 years to the birth year to find the year the person turns 18. For example, someone born in 2008 turns 18 in 2026.
3
The 18th birthday occurs on the same day and month as the date of birth, but in the year calculated in step 2.
4
To calculate the time remaining, subtract today's date from the 18th birthday date. This gives the number of years, months, and days left.

Worked Example

Date of Birth: September 5, 2008

18th Birthday Date: September 5, 2026

Today's Date: March 2024

Time Remaining: Approximately 2 years and 6 months

Result: The person turns 18 on September 5, 2026.

Quick Check

If you know the birth year, add 18 to find the year of the 18th birthday. The month and day remain the same.

Has the Birthday Passed?

If the month and day have already occurred in the current year, the person has already had their birthday this year. If not, the birthday is still ahead.
